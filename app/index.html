    The road to the platinum

    Roughly 200 hours from the opening mission to the last collectible. There is no mission replay, so the one missable trophy must be handled during the story — and the game gives you 15 manual save slots, so keep a safety save before the final missions.

    ⚠ The one missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida. No mission replay means a missed spot locks it for that playthrough. Sweep the spots early, before the endgame.

    1. Phase 1 — Story first ~60h

      Play the main story in order, chapters 1–4 through the final mission. Take stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) as their markers appear — eight full strands earn Strangers & Freaks. Along the way, meditate at every spot you pass: that is the only missable trophy.

    2. Phase 2 — Side activities ~50h

      Sample all 9 activity types, then chase the activity trophies: 250 stunts, 42 gold Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone-race wins, 20 race wins, fishing vs Cal, arcade, bowling, bank vaults, armored trucks, gang operations and the rest. Keep laundering Marked Bills and spending in shops — those totals tick up naturally.

    3. Phase 3 — Collectibles ~60h

      Work the map systematically: 3 Collectible Card sets, 100 WAiNK It photo spots, 100 vehicles discovered, 52 areas explored, all 8 garage properties, 10 Scooter Bros registrations. Use a checklist and clear one region at a time instead of criss-crossing the state.

    4. Phase 4 — Cleanup & 100% ~30h

      Mop up the remaining challenges (50 of them), any stray activity trophies, then check the pause-menu completion tracker. A Long Road (100% completion) is the last gate before the platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops.

    Cheats disable trophies — keep them off for the whole run.
